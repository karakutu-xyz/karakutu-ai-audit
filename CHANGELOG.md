A stopped audit is a terminal `cancelled` run with its recorded steps kept, no verdict, output or flags, and a story that reads "Stopped by a person"; the interrupted step shows as Stopped while its exact record stays unchanged, and late engine writes cannot replace it. Runs and articles filter by the new status, the shell shows queue state, and a reusable `StopRunButton` serves other views. Queue pause is in memory and resets on restart; shutdown still marks unfinished runs interrupted. Test mode stops DemoEngine runs mid-step and seeds one cancelled run.
